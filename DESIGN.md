# Design

## Direction

Reference-led dark scientific identity: near-black blue surfaces, luminous cyan accents, thin technical rules, generous negative space, light Inter Tight typography and high-contrast photographic or organic neural imagery.

## Foundations

- Font: supplied Inter Tight variable family.
- Content palette: deep blue-black canvas, white ink, muted blue-white, cyan action, restrained gray-blue rules.
- Layout: 12/8/4 column production grid with 48/32/16 px page margins and 24/24/16 px gutters.
- Spacing: mandatory XS through 3XL scale from the project-owned design-system source.

## Components

Production design-system families are Button, Text/Navigation Link, IconButton, Field and Choice. Section cards, circular process geometry, portrait frames and footer composition stay reference-owned.

## Motion

Behavior is mechanically ported from the frozen motion sandbox: staged motion readiness, masked heading lines, grouped slide reveals, hero graphics cover-parallax, media inner-layer parallax, four-state navigation and reduced-motion fallbacks.
