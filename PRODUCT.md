# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Researchers, clinical collaborators, institutions and people evaluating Synapse Neuroscience’s work in brain research and mental health.

## Product Purpose

Present Synapse Neuroscience’s research approach, measurable reach, multidisciplinary team and routes to collaborate. Success is a visitor understanding the organization’s work and choosing to explore or connect.

## Positioning

Synapse combines neuroscience and research to advance mental well-being.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: “Explore now”; closing CTA: “Connect with us”.
- The line a visitor remembers: “Combining neuroscience and research to advance mental well-being”.
- Belief ladder: scientific focus → credible process → measurable reach → multidisciplinary team → invitation to collaborate.
- Proof on hand: the four published metrics and “Partnered with MPI-P” shown in the supplied references.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, luminous and rigorous. The interface should feel focused, precise and optimistic about scientific progress.

## Anti-references

Do not introduce a generic light SaaS surface, stock-card dashboard language or imagery and decoration outside the supplied reference direction.

## Design Principles

- Let one coherent neural visual world carry the brand.
- Make the research process understandable without diluting its seriousness.
- Use evidence, collaboration and people as the progression toward trust.
- Preserve generous dark space and controlled cyan emphasis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, meaningful image alternatives, reduced motion and layouts without horizontal overflow from 320 CSS px.
